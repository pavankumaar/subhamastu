Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Subhamastu is a small personal wedding planner made by Pavan for family use. This page explains what information the app stores, why, and how you can have it deleted.

What we collect

  • Account details: your username and a securely hashed 4-digit PIN (the PIN itself is never stored).
  • If you use “Continue with Google”: your Google account ID, email address, name and profile-picture link. We do not get your Google password or access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Your planner: the tasks, payments, events, guest names, vendor details, notes and phone numbers you enter.
  • Mitra AI chats: the messages you send to the assistant and its replies.
  • Technical data: a sign-in token kept on your device so you stay logged in, and standard server logs (such as IP address) used for security and rate limiting.

How it is used

Only to run the planner for you: to sign you in, save and show your plan, and answer your Mitra AI requests. Each account can see only its own data. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for marketing.

Services that process your data

  • MongoDB Atlas — stores accounts, planner data and chat history.
  • Render — hosts the app's server.
  • here.now — hosts the website.
  • OpenRouter and the AI model it routes to — receive your Mitra AI messages, together with the planner details needed to answer them.
  • Google — only if you choose Google sign-in.
  • Your browser's speech service (Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari) — only if you tap the microphone; we receive just the resulting text.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. Sign-in sessions expire after 180 days. To delete your account and all of its data, or to ask what we hold about you, email pavan8985203872@gmail.com. We will act on the request within 30 days. If you connected Google, you can also remove Subhamastu's access at any time from your Google Account under “Third-party apps & services”.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), PINs are hashed, and repeated wrong PINs temporarily lock the account. Please don't store bank passwords, card numbers or other highly sensitive information in your notes.

Children

The app is meant for adults planning a wedding and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
